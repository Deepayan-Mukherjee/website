O Death! The Benevolent Equalizer

8 March 2025

Arose! Arose a stormy inky veil
of feathers ashen black!
A song of grief they sang
beckoning me to come back. A lifeless prop my eyes
Became; spiralled through all the shame
Of songs left unsung… trees left unclimbed
An echo through my mind whose decay never came. Through that sheet of obsidian
a hand of silken touch,
gestured signs of confelicity
those hands which laboured much. Pain and dread lay beyond the horizon
but I was at the shore singing…
the unsung… seeing the unseen.
Those endless waters of Styx so blessed and forgiving. That very shore a Kings opulent abode,
a peasant's shabby slum too,
a prisoner's cell, a sailor's ocean
everyone wearing an identical hue. That silken hand reached forward
it's form that of a child.
“I am Death.” that child said;
The tone was strangely comedic and mild. What's so funny Death? That cheerful smile
bowed seeds of bitterness within.
Your fear of me I don't understand
which can't be felt or seen.
When I am, you are not…
when you are, I am not.
Why did your God create heaven?
Because of me I'll tell you that. The clock struck the midnight hour
Bellowing Death's hollow chime
My conscious dissolving… it's only thought
O how fair though art!
