Becoming

14 November 2026

I heard them before dawn…
travelling. En route
to everywhere, and nowhere,
with them skies adorn.
A dome of ink,
a path beneath
the sound of steps
sound in sync.
The dome a golden ember,
a solar cacophony that path,
the yearning of steps hoping
to arrive, before the bleak December.
The disco ball eases off,
it’s cooling off,
its dimming.
Dimming.
Night.
