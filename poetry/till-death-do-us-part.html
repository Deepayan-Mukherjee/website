Till Death Do Us Part

25 July 2025

Atop an invisible peak
I breathe… Air too leaving me.
My callused soul felt meek,
none my witness! Not even me! In the heavens of my fabrication
there remains none the dweller,
solitude itself be my sole elation
It's warm embrace my propeller. Atop that invisible peak
my being itself may depart.
But you exist and caress my cheek…
Till Death Do Us Part! Suddenly a storm of silver
swept over that peak.
Within my eye I felt a glimmer
seeing a familiar physique,
atop that invisible peak. DEATH! I exclaimed solace
coursed through my veins.
Free from Solitude's embrace
It's burdening…damning chains. Death stood there my liberator
that eternal, everlasting existence…
turned for me into an obliterator.
With but a mere thought,
extinguished Solitude's presence. The silvery storm cooled off,
left behind a brilliant blue sky,
green trees where birds take off,
four-legged friends who come by. Atop that invisible peak
now a buoyant bustling place.
Things now don't look so bleak…
I live… basking in my own embrace.
